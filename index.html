Chun Jui Lu

Researcher in clinical machine learning
Director of Research, a community primary care clinic in New Taipei City, Taiwan · M.D., Chang Gung University

I study how machine learning can recover what routine clinical records leave out. Diagnoses in primary care claims are often incomplete, and the degree of incompleteness changes over time and across clinicians. My current work treats this as a weakly supervised learning problem: using laboratory evidence as partial ground truth to identify patients who likely have type 2 diabetes or chronic kidney disease but carry no recorded diagnosis.

The project draws on more than three decades of longitudinal records from a single community primary care clinic in New Taipei City, Taiwan.

Research interests

  • Positive-unlabeled and weakly supervised learning
  • Label noise and label-mechanism shift in real-world health data
  • Undiagnosed chronic disease detection in primary care
  • Longitudinal claims and electronic health record analysis

Current status

Research proposal stage. I welcome collaboration with researchers in machine learning, clinical epidemiology, and health services research.
