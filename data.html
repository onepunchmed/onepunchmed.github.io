Data

More than three decades of routine records from one community primary care clinic

Setting

A general practice clinic in New Taipei City, Taiwan, contracted with the National Health Insurance program and in continuous operation since 1992. Over this period the clinic has been staffed by several physicians in succession, which makes it possible to observe how recording practices change across clinical eras within the same patient population.

Coding systems

  • Diagnoses: recorded with clinic-internal short codes and mapped to ICD-10-CM for analysis. Early-period codes are coarser, so chronic conditions in the 1990s are likely under-represented.
  • Orders and medications: Taiwan National Health Insurance codes

Scale

About one million outpatient visits with prescription records from 1992 to 2026, of which roughly 0.86 million are linked to insurance claim records from 1995 onward.

Governance

  • Patient-level records do not leave the clinic. Analysis runs on a local read-only snapshot of the clinic system.
  • Only aggregate results leave the clinic, with small cells (fewer than 10 patients) suppressed or merged.
  • No individual patient or clinician is identified in any published material.
  • Ethics review: application in preparation.

Access

The data cannot be shared. Researchers interested in collaboration under an approved protocol are welcome to get in touch.

中文摘要：資料來自新北市一家自 1992 年開業的健保特約一般科診所，涵蓋門診、處方與醫令、健保申報，以及診所成人健檢與外送檢驗所的檢驗結果。1992 年起約一百萬筆門診處方紀錄，其中約 86 萬筆可連到 1995 年健保開辦後的申報資料。診斷以診所內部簡碼記錄、分析時對應到 ICD-10-CM，早期編碼較粗。病人層級資料不離開診所，分析在本地唯讀快照進行，對外只發表彙總結果並遮蔽小格數。資料無法分享，歡迎在核准的研究計畫下洽談合作。
